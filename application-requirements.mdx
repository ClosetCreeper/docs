title: "Application Requirements"
icon: "pencil"
---

In order to be eligible for us to review your server, please make sure you meet the following criteria:

- Have active members in your server daily
- Have a working support system
- Have active staff that are responsive
- Have at least 500 members
- Have no server drama at all
- Have a specific purpose (cannot be a general server)
- Have necessary channels for your needs, i.e. Rules, Media etc.
- Must be in compliance with Discord's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy as well as any other applicable terms (such as games, websites etc)
