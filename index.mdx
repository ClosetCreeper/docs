## About this website

This website aims to help you navigate our website and products as well as have information for partnering servers. Please view our helpful information below to see where you need to go!

**[Application Requirements](/application-requirements) -** If you are a server who is looking to certified with us, please review this documentation as it provides all the requirements necessary to get the process started.

**[Affiliate Requirements](/affiliate-requirements) -** If you are looking to get certified, also check this documentation, this provides more detailed information regarding certifications for different types of servers.

**[Small Server Program](/small-server-program) -** If you don't meet the eligibility requirements for our normal certification but have more than 100 active users, please look at our reduced requirements for small servers here.

**[Terms of Service](/terms-of-service) -** If you are looking for our rules and regulations regarding server, bot and service use, check them out here!

**[Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) -** If you want to know more information about how we store your data and keep it save, as well as how do request a removal of your data if required by applicable law, please check here!

**[Ascent Certification & Partnership Agreement](/partnership-agreement) -** Once you are a certified server, please review this documentation to learn how to stay in compliance with all of our policies and regulations (for more information about using our brand marks check this out as well!)
