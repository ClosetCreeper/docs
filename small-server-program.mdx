In order to qualify for our Small Server Program, you must meet all the criterion below. If you are not eligible, please view our [Affiliate Requirements](/application-requirements) for larger servers.

- Must have more than 100 members (not inclusive of bots)
- Must abide by Discord's [Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms)
- Must abide by Ascent's [Terms of Service](/terms-of-service)
- Must have an Affiliate channel in your server (see [Article 11 in our Agreement](/partnership-agreement))
- Must have an organized server in agreement with Discord's [Server Setup Guidelines](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/33023827550359-Discord-Server-Setup-Guide)
- Must have active staff members
- Must have a staff to member ratio of no lower than 1:4
