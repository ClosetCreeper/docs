## 1. Grant of Certification

1.1 Upon execution of this Agreement, Ascent grants the Partner server certified status, confirming that the Partner has, as of the Effective Date, met Ascent's Application Requirements. 

1.2 Certification is a designation reflecting compliance at the time of review and is not a permanent or irrevocable status. 

1.3 Ascent may issue a certification badge, listing, or similar mark for the Partner's use in accordance with Section 4 (Branding & Use of Marks).

## 2. Ongoing Compliance Obligations

2.1 The Partner agrees to maintain continuous compliance with Ascent's Application Requirements for the duration of their partnership. 

2.2 The Partner agrees to promptly notify Ascent of any sizable changes to server ownership, staff structure, moderation practices, or other factors relevant to the Application Requirements. 

2.3 Ascent may, at its discretion, conduct periodic reviews or audits to confirm continued compliance. 

2.4 The Partner agrees to cooperate with any reasonable review, audit, or information request from Ascent related to certification status.

## 3. Term & Review

3.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and remains in effect until terminated in accordance with Section 5. 

3.2 Ascent may conduct scheduled or unscheduled compliance reviews at its discretion. 

## 4. Branding & Use of Marks

4.1 While certified, the Partner may display Ascent's certification badge, logo, or related marks on their server or associated platforms, solely to indicate active certification status. 

4.2 Use of Ascent's name, marks, or branding must comply with any branding guidelines Ascent provides and must not misrepresent the scope or nature of certification. 

4.3 Upon termination of certification for any reason, the Partner must immediately cease all use of Ascent's certification badge, logo, marks, and any related promotional language. 

4.4 Ascent may ask you to remove branding, marks, or promotional materials at any time for any reason. Failure to comply will result in termination of this agreement and of the certification.

4.5 Continued use of Ascent's marks after termination constitutes a material breach of this Agreement and may result in further action.

## 5. Termination

5.1 **Termination for Non-Compliance.** Ascent may terminate the Partner's certification or partnership status at any time, with or without prior notice, if the Partner falls out of compliance with Ascent's Application Requirements, consistent with Section 12 of Ascent's Terms of Service. 

5.2 **Termination for Cause.** Ascent may terminate this Agreement immediately if the Partner breaches this Agreement, the Terms of Service, or engages in conduct that Ascent determines, in its discretion, harms Ascent, its users, or the integrity of the certification program. 

5.3 **Voluntary Termination.** The Partner may withdraw from certification or partnership at any time by notifying Ascent. 

5.4 **Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, certification status is revoked immediately, and the Partner must comply with Section 4.3 (removal of marks/badges). 

5.5 **Reapplication.** A Partner whose certification has been terminated may reapply for certification by submitting a new application and satisfying all current Application Requirements in full.

## 6. No Guarantee of Outcomes

6.1 Certification reflects compliance with Ascent's Application Requirements as assessed at the time of review and does not constitute a guarantee of server quality, safety, growth, or performance. 

6.2 Ascent makes no warranties, express or implied, regarding outcomes resulting from certification or partnership status.

## 9. Amendments

9.1 Ascent may update or amend the terms of this Agreement, the Application Requirements, or related policies at any time. 

9.2 Material changes will be communicated to certified Partners where reasonably practicable; continued participation in the certification program after such changes constitutes acceptance of the amended terms.
