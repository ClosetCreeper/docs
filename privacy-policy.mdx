This Privacy Policy explains how Ascent ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, stores, and protects information in connection with our website (ascentverify.xyz), our Discord server, and our bots and related services (together, "the Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

We collect as little information as we can in order to operate. Signing in with Discord gives us your Discord ID, username, display name, and avatar, and nothing else. We never ask Discord for your email or any sensitive information. We only collect your email and password if you choose to get verified. In addition, we don't sell your data, and we don't use advertising trackers.

---

## 1. Information we collect

1.1 **Discord account data** — when you sign up or sign in with Discord, we receive your Discord user ID, username, display name, and avatar. We only ask Discord for the "identify" permission, so Discord does not share your email address or any private information with us. (See [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy) for what Discord itself collects.)

1.2 **Verification data (optional)** — if you choose to get verified, you add an email address and a password. We confirm the email by sending you a link or code. Your password is stored by our authentication provider as a one-way hash.

1.3 **Ascent account data** — your Ascent ID, when your account was created and verified, how it was verified, any extra Discord accounts you link (their ID, username, display name, and avatar), and the color you choose for your card.

1.4 **Servers you set up** — when you add a server, we ask Discord once for the list of servers you're in, to show the ones you own or can manage. We only save the servers you choose to add. The data we save includes the server's ID, name, icon, member count, owner, the staff roles you pick, and the settings for each feature you turn on.

1.5 **Card sends** — when you send your card to a server, we record which card went to which server, the confirmation code, its status, and which staff member confirmed it.

1.6 **Verification records** — when someone verifies in a server through our bot, we record the server, their Discord ID, their Ascent ID, and the time.

1.7 **Billing data** — if you buy a plan, payment is handled by Stripe. We store your plan, its status and renewal date, and Stripe's customer and subscription IDs. We never see or store your card number.

1.8 **Summit requests** — if you use Summit (our workflow assistant), the description you type is used to build the workflow. We keep a count of how many builds each server uses per day. This does not include the prompt you used.

1.9 **Application and certification data** — information you submit in a certification or partnership application, including server details, staff information, and compliance documentation.

1.10 **Safety records** — if an account is banned, we keep the email and Discord IDs connected to it and the reason, so the ban can be enforced.

## 2. How our bot handles server data

2.1 Our bot works in Discord servers whose owners have added it. Server owners and their staff choose which features to turn on, and those settings decide what the bot does. This section applies to everyone in those servers, including people who have never used our website.

2.2 **Verification** — the bot gives and removes the roles a server has chosen when a member verifies.

2.3 **Moderation** — if a server turns on moderation, the bot checks new and edited messages against that server's rules (for example, blocked words or links) and can remove messages, time members out, kick or ban them.

2.4 **Logging** — if a server turns on logging, the bot posts events (such as edited or deleted messages, joins, leaves, and role changes) into a channel in that same server, chosen by its staff. These logs live in the server's Discord channel. Ascent does not copy or save message text into our own database.

2.5 **Workflows and custom profile** — if a server sets up workflows or a custom bot profile, we store those settings in order for the bot to act on them in the server.

2.6 To work, the bot reads what Discord makes available to it in each server, such as messages, members, and roles. It only acts in servers that have added it.

## 3. How we use information

We use collected information to:

3.1 Run the Service, including signing you in, showing and sending your card, and running the features servers turn on.

3.2 Verify accounts and let server staff confirm who you are when you send them your card.

3.3 Process payments and manage plans.

3.4 Process, review, and manage certification and partnership applications.

3.5 Detect, investigate, and prevent abuse, fraud, and security incidents, and enforce our [Terms of Service](/terms-of-service).

3.6 Contact you about your account, application, or certification status. We don't send marketing emails.

3.7 Comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Legal basis for processing

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, we process personal data on these legal bases:

4.1 **Contract** — to provide the Service you signed up for, including verification, card sends, and paid plans.

4.2 **Consent** — where you choose to give us something optional, such as an email for verification or an application. You can withdraw consent at any time.

4.3 **Legitimate interest** — for security, fraud prevention, and running server features that server owners turn on.

4.4 **Legal obligation** — where processing is required by law.

## 6. Data retention

6.1 We keep your account data while your account exists.

6.2 If you delete your account or ask us to, we delete your account data within 30 days, except where we need to keep something to enforce a ban, meet a legal obligation, or resolve a dispute.

6.3 Verification records and card-send history are kept for as long as the server uses Ascent, so servers can check who verified.

6.4 Application and certification records may be kept after a partnership ends, for audit and compliance history.

6.5 Billing records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.

## 7. Cookies and browser storage

7.1 We use your browser's storage to keep you signed in and to remember small preferences, such as your cookie choice and whether the sidebar is collapsed. These are essential and can't be turned off.

7.2 We ask before using anything non-essential, such as analytics, through the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time with **Manage cookies**. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

## 10. International data transfers

10.1 Our database is in the United States, and our providers may process data in other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as our providers' standard contractual clauses, for these transfers.

## 11. Data security

11.1 All traffic to the website and our services uses HTTPS.

11.2 Passwords are stored as one-way hashes by our authentication provider, and are never visible to us.

11.3 Database access rules limit each signed-in user to their own data. Server settings can only be changed by the server's owner and the staff they allow.

11.4 Sending your card to a server needs a one-time code sent to your email, and server staff need a separate code from you to open it.

11.5 Our database provider encrypts stored data.

11.6 No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.
