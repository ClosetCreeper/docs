This Privacy Policy explains how Ascent ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information in connection with our website, Discord server, and associated bots/services (collectively, "the Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

1.1 **Account & Identity Data** — your Discord user ID, username, and associated profile information provided via Discord authentication. (view [Discord Policy](https://discord.com/privacy) for more)

1.2 **Server (Guild) Data** — the Discord servers you are a member of, including server IDs, server names, and your role(s) and channel access within those servers, as made available through Discord's API.

1.3 **Application & Certification Data** — information submitted as part of a certification or partnership application, including server details, staff information, and compliance documentation.

1.4 **Website Account Data** — if you create an account on our website, we may collect an email address, username, and authentication credentials.

1.5 **Usage Data** — command usage, timestamps, and interaction logs collected for moderation, security, and service improvement purposes.

1.6 **Technical Data** — IP address, browser type, and device information collected automatically when you use our website.

1.7 **Server, role, and channel data (Sections 1.1–1.2)** is collected because it is essential to the core functionality of the Service — including verifying server ownership or staff status, displaying eligible servers for certification, and enforcing role-based access on the website. This data is retrieved via Discord's API and reflects only what your Discord account already has access to; we do not gain access beyond what Discord's permission scopes grant.

2.1 Operate, maintain, and improve the Service.
